## Get started in three steps

<Steps>

1. **Check your machine.** A Linux box you can run commands on as root: Debian 12 or 13, or Ubuntu 24.04, on x86 or ARM. [What you need](/start/requirements/)

2. **Connect it.** Make a join token in the app and paste one command on the machine. It installs everything and links it in about five minutes. [Connect your first node](/install/first-node/)

3. **Deploy a server.** Pick a game, name it, and Raptor installs and starts it. Give your players its address. [Deploy your first server](/install/first-server/)

</Steps>

Your first machine is free for a month, then $12 a month each. [Pricing](/start/pricing/)
