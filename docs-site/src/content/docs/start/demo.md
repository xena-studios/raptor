The demo is a real Raptor Panel with real game servers on a real machine: the console streams, the graphs move, and add-on search works. Open it from **Try the demo** on [raptorpanel.net](https://raptorpanel.net), or go to [app.raptorpanel.net/demo](https://app.raptorpanel.net/demo). There's a quick security check, then **Start the demo**. You don't need an email or a passkey.

If the page says "The demo isn't open right now", it's being set up or looked after. Try again later.

## What you can do

You get an hour as a guest in the demo's org, on its showcase servers (a Paper server with plugins, a Fabric server with mods, and Uptime Kuma). You can:

- Watch the console and send commands, and start and stop servers.
- Browse and edit files, and search and install add-ons.
- Make and run schedules.
- Take backups and look inside them.
- Run the connection test, and see the activity.
- Change settings like memory.

## What's off

Other visitors share the same servers, so the demo turns off what could break them for everyone or reach outside Raptor. Trying one of these says **"That's off in the demo: in your own Raptor, you can do it."**

- Anything your passkey signs in your own Raptor: making and deleting servers, wipe and reinstall, changing a server's image, and restoring backups. Those buttons say **"In your own Raptor, your passkey signs this."**
- SFTP, **Upload → From a link**, and importing from another host.
- External database access, networks, the firewall, and node settings.
- Billing, members and invitations, support access, sharing logs to mclo.gs, and deleting the org.
- Changing your account: there's no email or passkey to change.

## Resets

A banner on every page says **"You're in the demo. It resets every hour."** Every hour, guests are signed out and the servers' trash is emptied. Your hour starts when you open the demo, so you might be signed out sooner. Start again from the demo page to get another hour.

Don't put anything private in the demo: other visitors can see the servers' files and consoles.

When you're ready for your own, [connect your first node](/install/first-node/).
