The command menu finds everything you can open or do in Raptor: every page, every server and node, the egg library, your org's members, and actions like restarting a server or whitelisting a player. Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) on any page, or **/** when you're not typing in a field, or click **Search…** in the top bar.

## Finding things

Start typing. You don't need the exact name: `surv` finds Survival, `mcp` finds Minecraft: Paper, and small typos like `survivl` still match. You can also search by a server's game, its node, or its address. Words can be in any order, so `restart survival` and `survival restart` find the same thing.

Results are grouped (the page you're on, Actions, Servers, Nodes, Pages, Eggs, Members), the best match first. What you've used recently and often ranks higher. The last result always searches these docs for what you typed.

With nothing typed, the menu shows what you've pinned, the server or node you're looking at, what you used recently, and the most common actions.

## Doing things

Pick an action and the menu walks you through it. **Send a console command**, for example, asks which server, then for the command. The steps show at the top; **Backspace** in an empty search, or **Esc**, goes back one.

On a server's page, that server's tabs and actions come first: restart it, open its console or files, back it up, run one of its schedules, update all its add-ons, whitelist, op, or kick a player (Minecraft Java), copy its address, open its web dashboard, share its log on mclo.gs, or test its connection. On a node's page, its tabs, deploying a server to it, and copying its hostname come first.

Highlight a server, node, or egg and press **Tab** to see everything you can do with it.

**Stop** and **Kill** ask before they act. Updating add-ons shows the list on the Add-ons tab before anything changes, and **Invite a member** fills in the email on Members for you to pick their role.

You only see what you're allowed to do. A member who can't use a server's power controls won't find Restart in the menu either, and a stopped server offers Start, not Stop.

## Pins and recents

Press **Alt+P** (**⌥P** on a Mac) on a highlighted result to pin it to the top of the menu, and again to unpin it. Pins and recent items are kept in this browser only.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Ctrl+K** or **⌘K** | Open or close the command menu, from anywhere |
| **/** | Open it, when you're not typing in a field |
| **↑** **↓** | Move through the results |
| **Enter** | Open or run the highlighted result |
| **Tab** | A server's, node's, or egg's own actions |
| **Backspace** | Back a step, when the search is empty |
| **Esc** | Back a step; at the start, close the menu |
| **Alt+P** or **⌥P** | Pin or unpin the highlighted result |
| **?** | Show these shortcuts, when the search is empty |
| **Ctrl+B** or **⌘B** | Show or hide the sidebar |

The file editor has its own shortcuts, listed under the file manager's settings button.
